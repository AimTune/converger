# 2026-10-15, a8dd704: rolling restart with 10 000 sockets

This run measures the exit criterion of [#58](https://github.com/AimTune/converger/issues/58) that was tracked in
[#190](https://github.com/AimTune/converger/issues/190): **a rolling restart under 10 000 connections loses no
acknowledged message**. The application code is `main` at `d23f8ce`; `a8dd704` adds the gradual connect
(`LOAD_RESTART_CONNECT_RATE`) and the restart delay (`LOAD_RESTART_DELAY_S`) to the harness.

```bash
LOAD_NODES=3 LOAD_SCENARIOS=rolling_restart LOAD_CONVERSATIONS=1000 LOAD_SUBSCRIBERS=10 LOAD_RATE=0.1 \
  LOAD_RESTART_CONNECT_RATE=100 LOAD_RESTART_DELAY_S=130 LOAD_WARMUP_S=0 load/run.sh
```

1 000 conversations with 10 sockets each connect at 100 sockets/s, which takes 100 s. Every conversation then gets a
REST message every 10 s (100 messages/s in total, 1 000 WebSocket frames/s). After 130 s, `run.sh` restarts the three
nodes one after the other with SIGTERM: readiness flips, sockets drain with close 1012, and the node comes back. Each
of the 10 000 sockets resumes from its last `seq` and must end with every `seq` of its conversation exactly once.

It ran on the same developer machine as the [first run](2026-10-12-612ec15.md): Windows 11 with Docker Desktop
(WSL 2), 12 vCPUs and 31 GiB, shared by the three nodes, Postgres, k6 and the mock provider. Nothing else was running
in Docker; other desktop applications were open.

## Findings

- **No acknowledged message was lost.** All 10 000 sockets were verified: 21 250 messages acked, 0 lost, 0 duplicate
  `seq`, 0 never acked. Every message also reached the outbound webhook and the backbone subscriber exactly once.
- **Reconnects are far slower than the 30 s target of [ADR-0027](../adr/0027-websocket-limits-backpressure-and-draining.md).**
  The time from close to `welcome` was p50 30.0 s, p95 93.6 s and max 175.5 s. When a node drains, its ~3 300
  sockets move to the two other nodes. Every handshake and every watermark replay reads the database, so the
  20-connection pools saturated (1.7 s queue wait at the peak). Requests were answered with retryable errors
  (3 449 `replay_unavailable`, 42 307 REST send retries), and client backoff stretched the tail. This is tracked in
  [#198](https://github.com/AimTune/converger/issues/198).
- Nodes were ready again 27–38 s after their restart, and REST → WebSocket latency during the run was p50 746 ms,
  p95 2.3 s.
- One machine ran the whole cluster, Postgres and the load generator, so absolute numbers are pessimistic. The
  zero-loss result does not depend on that.
