# 2026-10-15: partition archive and drop at 100M rows

This run measures the exit criterion of [#59](https://github.com/AimTune/converger/issues/59) that was tracked in
[#192](https://github.com/AimTune/converger/issues/192): **partition drop and archive verified on a 100M-row dataset**.
The command was `test/load/partition_100m/run.sh` with its defaults ([how it works](index.md#partition-archive-and-drop-at-100m-rows)).

## Findings

- **Verified at 100M rows.** 100 013 776 rows (60M `activities`, 40M `deliveries`, 6 months, 8 tenants; 54.6 GiB).
  `Converger.Retention.run/1` handled the oldest month (16 666 667 rows), with the production code and settings:
  it detached the partitions concurrently, exported them to MinIO, read the export back, verified it and dropped them.
  The 336 archive objects (1.45 GiB) were downloaded again, and their SHA-256 hashes and per-tenant row counts matched
  the partition counted before the detach. One tenant re-imported with matching counts.
- **Writers were not blocked.** 8 writers inserted 25 rows/s each through `Activities.create_activity/2` the whole time,
  with 0 failed inserts. Their p99 was 144 ms in the baseline and 124 ms during the run. During the detach it was
  186 ms, and during the drop 129 ms; the maximum over the whole run was 309 ms.
- **Detach and drop stay constant.** `DETACH PARTITION ... CONCURRENTLY` took 205 ms and `DROP TABLE` 14 ms for both
  tables. Export and verification are what grow with the month: 530 s for 16.7M rows, about 31 000 rows/s. That is
  slower than the 57 000–59 000 rows/s at 10M–30M rows in #196's run, because this month did not fit in Postgres'
  2 GiB of shared buffers.
- Conditions: a developer machine (Ryzen 5 7600X, Docker Desktop on Windows, 12 CPUs, 31 GiB for Docker). During the
  seed, a load test from another worktree ran in Docker at the same time, so the seed time is pessimistic. The
  retention-phase numbers are within the range of the quiet smoke runs.
