# 2026-10-12, 612ec15: first run, 1 and 3 nodes

The first full run of the [load harness](index.md), `full` profile, on the native backbone. The application code is
`main` at `92e21a1`; `612ec15` only adds the harness. This is the **pre-scale baseline** that
[#34](https://github.com/AimTune/converger/issues/34) asks to compare against after
[#35](https://github.com/AimTune/converger/issues/35). The Kafka column comes with
[#66](https://github.com/AimTune/converger/issues/66).

It ran on a developer machine: Windows 11 with Docker Desktop (WSL 2), 12 vCPUs and 31 GiB visible to Docker. Every
node, Postgres, k6 and the mock provider shared these, with no per-node limits. In the 3-node topology the three
nodes also share one host and one Postgres (3 × 20 pooled connections). That topology therefore measures clustering
overhead and failover, not horizontal scale. These numbers are not comparable with the nightly CI runs.

## Findings

- **No acknowledged message was lost** in any scenario on either topology. This held for 1 000 sockets under load,
  reconnect storms of all 1 000 sockets, graceful restarts of every node, and a SIGKILL. It also covers every webhook
  delivery (8 634 and 6 789 burst deliveries, 23 820 and 27 382 fan-out deliveries).
- **REST → WebSocket p95 misses the 250 ms target**: 433 ms on 1 node at 94 msgs/s with 1 000 sockets. The REST
  POST itself takes as long (432 ms p95), so the write path is the bottleneck, not the WebSocket fan-out.
- **The DB pool saturates under bursts.** At 500 inbound webhooks/s, one node with `POOL_SIZE=20` accepted about
  96/s. The rest got HTTP **500**: Ecto dropped the queued connection checkouts after about 100 ms. Converger should
  answer 503 or 429 with `Retry-After` and flip readiness instead, which is the subject of #35. WebSocket handshakes
  also read the database: in an earlier attempt at 500 connects/s, only 2 579 of 10 000 sockets got in (the harness
  now connects at 100/s and clients back off).
- **Outbound delivery throughput is low**: about 85 deliveries/s with a mock provider that answers in 20–30 ms and 20
  delivery workers, where about 700/s would be expected. After the burst, the p50 delivery took 42 s.
- **Idle sockets cost about 130–150 KiB each** (RSS growth for 10 000 sockets: 1.9 GB on one node). That is more
  than the "tens of KiB" in the capacity guidance.
- Nodes come back ready 20–30 s after a restart or kill (42 s at most on 3 nodes). During a 3-node rolling restart,
  the slowest client needed 51 s to reconnect: its backoff kept landing on nodes that were restarting or busy.
